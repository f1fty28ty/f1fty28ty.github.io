Backside 180

Spin Beginner Air Switch Landing
▶ Regular Stance
▶ Goofy Stance

Step-by-Step Execution

01 Approach and Setup +
Ride toward the feature in a centered stance, speed matched to kicker height. Shoulders parallel to the board. No pre-rotation — stay neutral until the wind-up. Weight should be distributed evenly over both feet, not back.
02 Wind-Up +
Two to three strides out, rotate your upper body toeside — away from the fall line. 45° is enough. Riders who over-wind lose edge pressure and blow the pop. The wind-up must complete before you reach the lip, not at it. Mark a spot on your approach.
03 Pop from the Tail +
Drive the back foot hard into the tail as you reach the lip. The pop triggers the rotation — the wind-up stored the energy, the pop releases it. Late pop kills the trick every time. If you're muscling the spin, the pop is late.
04 Lead with the Head +
Head turns first. Look over your backside shoulder immediately at takeoff. The body follows the head. If you wait to look, you wait to spin. This is the single habit that separates riders who learn this in one session from those who take a week.
05 Let the Board Catch Up +
At 90°, your upper body is done rotating — hold it. The board and lower body continue coming around. Don't rush the hips. Halfway through, board and body should be aligned. If your board is lagging, your wind-up was too short.
06 Spot and Absorb +
Find your landing before you get there. Bend the knees on impact, weight centered over the board. You will land switch — this is the point. Ride it out. If you can't ride away switch comfortably, practice switch before adding this trick to your repertoire.

Coach's Corner — Common Mistakes

Pop before wind-up is complete

Fix: The wind-up must finish before you reach the lip, not at it. Mark a spot on your approach — wind-up must be done there. If you can't tell when you're winding up, film it.

Weight back at takeoff

Fix: Back-weighted approaches collapse the pop. You can't drive the tail if your hips are behind your feet. Ride tall, stay centered. This shows up as nose-high landings and incomplete rotation.

Under-rotating, landing nose-heavy

Fix: Almost always a pop timing error, not a rotation problem. Film the pop. Fix the pop. Rotation comes for free once the pop is right.
